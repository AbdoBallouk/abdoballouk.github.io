Damascus — Syria · On-site, bilingual EN/AR

More than 15 years in R&D and university teaching focused on radio-frequency (RF) signal analysis and spectrum management. I design signal-reliability solutions for communications under complex RF conditions using software-defined radio (SDR) platforms; develop experimental frameworks to assess signal behaviour in UAV systems and satellite-navigation (GNSS) systems; and research ways to make critical wireless infrastructure and air-navigation systems more resilient.

Research leadership: peer-reviewed publications on signal reliability in real, complex environments with practical applications to civil infrastructure, multi-disciplinary experimental communications teams, and hands-on experience in high-power RF systems and compact design. Engineering delivery: 8+ projects led end-to-end — from electronic circuit design and PCB manufacturing through high-power RF stages to software, calibration and field testing.
